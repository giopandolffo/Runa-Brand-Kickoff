# RUNA — Brand Identity System

## Product Purpose

RUNA is a sports/streetwear brand that starts on the mat (jiu-jitsu) and extends to surf, snow, athletics, and the street. Born from the Runa Uruz symbol — representing primitive strength, endurance, and new beginnings.

## Users

- Athletes of martial arts and contact sports (BJJ, MMA, boxing)
- Streetwear-conscious fitness enthusiasts
- Community-driven individuals who value discipline, respect, and growth
- Latinoamerican market starting with Chile

## Brand Archetype

The Outlaw + The Ruler. Disruptive yet authoritative. Built on respect, not aggression.

## Tone / Voice

- **DIRECT.** Short, punchy sentences. No fluff.
- **ATHLETIC.** Speaks to the grind, the mat, the friction.
- **REAL.** Honest about where RUNA starts (jiu-jitsu mat) and where it's going.
- English for brand taglines ("Built for battle", "You belong here", "Forged in friction")
- Spanish for local communication
- Never corporate jargon ("innovador", "disruptivo", "sinergia")
- Never aggressive or intimidating
- Never cliché gym talk ("no pain no gain")

## Taglines

- "Built for battle." — Brand anchor
- "You belong here." — Community
- "Forged in friction." — Movement
- "Together, we are stronger." — Strength
- "Tap or be tapped." — JJF Collab
- "The mat doesn't lie." — JJF Collab
- "Leave it on the mat." — JJF Collab
- "Friction is the forge." — Drop 02

## Brand Pillars

1. **Crecimiento (Growth)** — Self-improvement, confidence. Every training is a step forward.
2. **Comunidad (Community)** — People and belonging. RUNA is a tribe.
3. **Equilibrio (Balance)** — Body, mind, spirit. Performance without balance isn't sustainable.
4. **Respeto (Respect)** — Values, discipline, humility. Respect the rival, the partner, yourself.

## Design Philosophy

- Think outside the box — away from traditional BJJ visual codes
- Flow and elegance — typography with movement
- Visual power — immediate impact, desire to belong
- "Crear una identidad poderosa pero cercana, única, fresca y reconocible"

## Origin

The Runa Uruz (ᚢ) represents strength, endurance, and new beginnings. The logo transforms this ancestral symbol into modern typography with flow, connecting past and future.

## Competitors / Anti-references

- Traditional BJJ brands (too cliché)
- Generic gym wear
- Corporate sportswear (Nike, Adidas — different scale)
- Brands that pretend to be global when they're local
